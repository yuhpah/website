History of St. John's Cathedral

St. John's Anglican Cathedral is located in Belize City. It is one of the most important historical landmarks in Belize.

Construction of the church began in 1812 and was completed around 1820. The bricks used to build the church were brought from England by ships.

The church was originally a parish church. In 1891, it became St. John's Cathedral. It is known as the oldest Anglican church in Central America.

The cathedral also has a long connection with the history of Belize. Four kings of the Mosquito Coast were crowned there between 1815 and 1845.

Three Highlights From My Trip

1. Seeing the Old Building

My first highlight was seeing the old brick building. It was interesting to see a structure that has been in Belize for more than 200 years.

2. Learning About the History

My second highlight was learning about the history of St. John's Cathedral and its importance to Belize.

3. Seeing the Inside of the Cathedral

My third highlight was seeing the inside of the cathedral. I enjoyed looking at the wooden furniture, windows and other historical features.
